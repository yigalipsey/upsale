# Content Quality Findings — upsaleil.com

## Summary Score: 40/100

---

## Finding 1: No Dedicated About Page [HIGH]

`/about` returns HTTP 404. The only company information is a ~100-word inline section on the homepage, with no named founders, team credentials, or company history.

**Impact:** Severe E-E-A-T signal weakness. Google's Quality Rater Guidelines emphasize clear "who is responsible for the website" information. A B2B service without an About page lacks credibility signals.

**Fix:** Create `/about` page with founder names, team bios with LinkedIn links, company founding story, mission, and physical address.

---

## Finding 2: No Author Credentials [HIGH]

All 11 article pages attribute content to "Upsale Team" with no named authors, biographical information, LinkedIn profiles, or professional qualifications.

**Impact:** Articles lack individual expertise signals. In a YMYL-adjacent industry (business consulting), author credentials significantly impact ranking potential.

**Fix:** Assign named authors to each article. Add author bios with professional qualifications and LinkedIn links.

---

## Finding 3: Unsubstantiated Marketing Claims [HIGH]

Multiple statistical claims appear without evidence:
- "40+ small businesses increased sales with us"
- "25% average increase in sales within 3 months"
- "Over 10 years of experience"

No client testimonials, named case studies, logos, or third-party verification appear anywhere on the site.

**Impact:** Google's E-E-A-T framework penalizes unsubstantiated claims. Users have no reason to trust these numbers.

**Fix:** Add named client testimonials, case studies with verifiable results, or third-party review platform links.

---

## Finding 4: No Physical Business Address [HIGH]

No physical address is listed anywhere on the site. Only a WhatsApp number (053-335-0910) and email. Israeli commercial websites are legally required to display business address and company registration details.

**Impact:** Legal non-compliance. Weakens LocalBusiness SEO signals. Reduces user trust.

**Fix:** Add business address to footer and contact section. Include company registration number if applicable.

---

## Finding 5: Very Thin Content on Article Subpages [HIGH]

`/services/Telemeeting/article-1` has only ~180-220 words. Other article-1/2/3 subpages range from 400-500 words — below competitive thresholds.

**Impact:** Thin pages struggle to rank for target keywords. Risk of being classified as "low quality" by Google.

**Fix:** Expand all article subpages to minimum 600 words with substantive, unique content.

---

## Finding 6: Thin Service Pages [MEDIUM]

`/services/sales-outsourcing` and `/services/Telemeeting` each have ~400-500 words with minimal depth (2 H2s each, most content in feature boxes).

**Fix:** Add 300+ words of original service detail per page with proper heading hierarchy.

---

## What Works

- Homepage has ~800-1000 words of unique, relevant content
- FAQ section with 10 well-chosen questions
- Article topics are relevant to the target audience
- Contact form present on all pages
